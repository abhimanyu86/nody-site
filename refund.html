Refund policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

We want you to be happy with Nody. There is no free trial, so the refund window is how you find out whether he suits you and your Mac.

14 days, any reason

If you bought Nody and it is not working out, write to motormaniac10@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase, from the email address you bought with or with your order number. You do not need to give a reason, though hearing what went wrong helps us fix it.

How it works

After 14 days

If Nody stops working on a Mac that meets the requirements and we cannot fix it, write to us. We will look at it case by case.

Nothing here limits refund rights that the law where you live gives you.
