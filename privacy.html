Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Nody is an app that runs on your Mac. It is made and sold by Abhimanyu Malik ("we"). This page says what data Nody handles, where it goes, and what we receive. The short version: what you say to Nody is processed on your Mac, and we do not run servers that receive it.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your voice. The microphone is listened to on your Mac so Nody can hear his wake phrase and your requests. Speech is turned into text by a model running on your Mac. Audio is not sent to us or stored, apart from the most recent few seconds, which are kept in a file on your Mac for troubleshooting and overwritten each time.
  • The voice lock. If you turn it on, a numeric voiceprint made from your samples is stored on your Mac and compared there.
  • Your requests and his answers. By default they are answered by a language model running on your Mac. A history of conversations is kept on your Mac so you can look back; you can clear it in Settings.
  • Things he keeps for you: reminders, lists, notes, follow-ups, routines, what you asked him to remember, and an index of the document folders you chose. All are files in a folder in your home directory (~/.openjarvis). Deleting that folder deletes them.
  • Your screen. When you ask him to look at your screen, a screenshot is taken and read on your Mac.
  • Meeting notes. When you start meeting notes, what the microphone hears is transcribed on your Mac. Other people may be audible; you are responsible for telling them and for following the recording laws where you are.

Nody contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. The open-source OpenJarvis runtime that Nody installs has optional usage analytics of its own; Nody's installer turns them off.

What we receive

  • When you buy. Payment is handled by Dodo Payments, which is the merchant of record. They collect your payment details; we never see your card. We receive your name, email address, country, what you bought and when, so that we can deliver the licence, give support and keep accounts.
  • When you write to us. Your email address and what you send, including any log files you choose to attach. Logs can contain the text of your requests, so look before you send.
  • This website. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested). The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Support email is kept for up to two years. You can ask us to delete support correspondence sooner.

Your choices and rights

You can use Nody with no second brain and no Google sign-in. You can clear his history and memory in Settings, and delete everything by removing ~/.openjarvis. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you (your purchase and support records). Write to motormaniac10@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

Nody is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
